About

A developer who likes finishing things

Most of what I build starts as a question — can this recognize a face, triage a symptom, verify uptime without a middleman — and ends as something a real person can use.

I work across the stack: React and TypeScript on the front end, Node and Python behind it, and enough time in Blender and Figma to care about how things actually look and feel. My background spans web development, game development and 3D modelling, and it shows up as a habit of treating interface, logic and data as one connected problem rather than three separate jobs.

I write code I'm willing to hand to someone else, and I'd rather ship a smaller feature that works cleanly than a large one that half-works. Outside of client work, I'm usually reading through an open-source codebase or rebuilding something I used last week just to understand it better.
